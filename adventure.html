01 · Introduction

Built for the terrain wheelchairs aren't.

Traditional wheelchairs are optimized for sidewalks, pavement, and indoor flooring. But many users — especially in low- and middle-income communities — regularly face dirt roads, gravel, mud, sand, potholes, and hills, where standard wheelchairs become exhausting and unsafe.

The ELEOS Adventure project is a senior capstone in partnership with ELEOS Solutions: a wheelchair platform that handles rough terrain while remaining affordable, manufacturable from off-the-shelf parts, easy to reproduce, and practical for real-world users.

02 · Problem Statement

The gap.

Off-road wheelchair systems already exist — but most are prohibitively expensive, depend on custom-machined parts, or sacrifice off-road capability to keep costs down. The result: communities that need durable mobility most have the least access to it. This project closes that gap with a wheelchair that maximizes off-road capability using standard, commercially-available components.

04 · Design Details

Six features. One ride.

Built around standard bike components and simple fabrication, so future teams can iterate and reproduce locally.

01

Lever-Drive Propulsion

Long drive levers give the user dramatically more mechanical advantage than pushrims alone — easier hill climbing, stronger strokes, and less fatigue on long distances or rough terrain.

Hill-climb optimized High torque
02

Detachable Levers

Quick-detach mechanism lets the rider clip the levers onto the frame when not in use, switch to pushrim operation, or operate in reverse. Storage, transport, and indoor use all stay simple.

Quick-detach Reverse capable
03

Bike Tires + Pushrims

Large bicycle tires roll over potholes and rough ground with far better traction than standard wheelchair tires. Integrated pushrims preserve traditional wheelchair use — fully usable indoors and in tight spaces.

Dual-mode Better traction
04

Disc Brakes

Bike-style disc brakes replace unreliable pushrim friction stops. The critical benefit: preventing rollback between propulsion strokes on hills — keeping climbs efficient and safe on wet or loose surfaces.

Anti-rollback All-terrain stop
05

Dog Gear Quick-Release

A custom nylon dog gear lets the rear wheels pop off in seconds — for storage, transport, or maintenance. Nylon was chosen for its strength, durability, and suitability for injection molding at scale.

Nylon Mass-prod ready
06

Large Front Caster

A larger-than-typical front caster wheel — sized to roll over the rocks, ruts, and potholes that stop small wheelchair casters cold. Better stability, better steering, better terrain adaptability.

Obstacle-ready Stable
05 · Test Results

Cut the time in half.

Time trials across three terrain types, with three distinct participant groups: children, adult males, and adult females. Times are total traversal time per terrain, lower is better.

Terrain Adventure (s) Standard Wheelchair (s) Improvement
Uphill54.9106.449%
Dirt16.633.150%
Pothole21.724.912%

The strongest gains came from hill climbing and dirt — validating the lever-drive propulsion and larger wheel system. Pothole improvement was smaller but still meaningful, confirming better obstacle handling.

07 · Why It Matters

Mobility is more than transport.

For many users, mobility directly shapes access to education, employment, healthcare, and community life. An affordable, off-road-capable wheelchair isn't a piece of gear — it's a path to independence.

The ELEOS Adventure is a starting point: a proven, reproducible platform that real communities can afford, repair, and build on.
